The Podcast

A Straight Line from CWD's Research Desk to Your Ears

The CIO's Diary takes the same CIO-level vantage point behind every CWD research note, built on independent economic and geopolitical research, market strategists, financial news services and academic research, and compresses it into a weekly conversation. Every episode assumes you already know the vocabulary, so there's no time lost to explaining it.

01

What Moved

A data-dense rundown of what actually moved markets this week. No term definitions, no throat-clearing, straight to the numbers that mattered.

02

What Should Have Moved

The core segment, and the one that gets the most airtime: the gap between what should have driven markets and what actually did, and why that gap exists.

03

What's Next

Specific, attributed predictions for the week ahead, plus one technical or positioning takeaway active investors can actually use.

Who's Behind the Mic

Two Voices, One Weekly Discipline

No outside guests. The same two people, every week, which keeps the show tight and keeps the research honest.

Chris Darbyshire
CIO & Founder, CWD Consulting

With an outstanding career spanning over two decades in investment management, Chris brings the same institutional-quality macro analysis and disciplined asset allocation thinking he applies to every CWD client engagement. On the show, he's the CIO-level lens on what actually mattered this week.

Michael
Host

Michael interviews Chris every week, pushing past the headline data toward the practical, portfolio-relevant takeaway, so the conversation stays grounded in what active investors can actually do with it.
