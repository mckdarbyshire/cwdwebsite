What each webinar covers

Each week Chris draws on a wide range of the world's leading research houses, macro strategists and independent economists, and filters it through an institutional CIO lens. The webinar brings that together in one focused session.

Macro top-down briefing

The week's main economic, political and trade-related events, with the charts and data behind them.

Cross-asset market update

A review of the asset classes used in multi-asset portfolios: equities, bonds, currencies, commodities and alternatives.

Thematic updates

The themes driving markets right now, such as AI financing, tariffs and geopolitics, and what they mean for portfolios.

Tactical investment ideas

Ideas explained from conception to execution, so your investment committee can weigh them on their merits.
